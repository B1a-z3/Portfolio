Risk modeling · Decision policy · Experiment design

Used-Car Auction Bid Strategy

Which used cars go bad, and what should we bid? I scored bad-buy risk on 72,983 auction purchases with XGBoost (AUC 0.767 against a 0.750 logistic baseline), leaving out price and post-purchase fields to prevent leakage. The risk became a maximum-bid rule, back-tested out of time on 14,470 cars.

Along the way I caught a confounder (overpayment risk disappears once you control for vehicle age) and designed an A/B test with power analysis from 6,400 simulated regressions.

NBA Performance & Game Outcome Prediction

One pipeline, two models: team-level game outcomes and player-level decline risk. A PostgreSQL warehouse is loaded by a Kaggle backfill plus incremental nba_api pulls, with a shared validation layer and pytest checks for look-ahead leakage.

The first XGBoost pass barely beat logistic regression. A SQL finding that back-to-back teams shoot worse led to rest-day asymmetry features, which produced the real accuracy lift. Models are served through FastAPI and scored weekly on accuracy, log-loss and Brier score.

Research · Clustering · Uncertainty quantification

Rock Typing + NMR Logging for Permeability

Research at UT Arlington on the Arbuckle carbonate aquifer in Kansas. A single global model predicted permeability worse than the mean, so I first grouped rock into eight hydraulic flow units with K-means on the Flow Zone Indicator, then calibrated a separate model for each.

The training set came from depth-matching 7,000+ NMR well-log rows to 694 lab core plugs across two wells, giving 472 one-to-one matched samples. Coefficient uncertainty is quantified with OLS and 10,000-sample bootstrapping.
